A castle on the watch

Walk through the gate passage and courtyard, then take the timber stair across the courtyard to the wall-walk. The hall and towers remain closed.

This gatehouse evokes late-thirteenth-century western Europe, with Welsh and Marcher castles as references. It is an illustration, not a surveyed reconstruction.

Sources & materials

Original Blender geometry. Stone and timber textures from Poly Haven (CC0). Full asset credits accompany the source package.

Portcullis reference: English Heritage, Goodrich Castle teachers’ kit, pages 24 and 38.

Choose Begin watch to start a short alarm scene. Sound begins only with that action; the scene also works silently. Replay returns the same defenders to their starting positions before beginning again.

The defenders are original stylized characters. Clothing evokes the period; the horn is a narrative prop, not a claim about routine equipment in a particular garrison.

Controls

Use W A S D or drag the thumb pad to walk. Release to stop. Drag the view or use the arrow keys to look. On a phone, use one thumb on the pad and the other on the view. There is no jumping or sprinting. Tap a gate or door to select it, then use its action. Oak doors operate together. The compact Defences menu provides a keyboard-accessible alternative for visible mechanisms. Activate the action again to reverse movement. System reduced-motion preferences are respected.
